# SOP-C-202: Coolant filter clog: differential pressure high

## 1 Symptoms

Filter differential pressure above 0.7 bar and rising; outlet pressure falling while inlet pressure stays normal; coolant flow gradually dropping.

## 2 Diagnosis

High filter differential pressure with normal inlet pressure means the cartridge is loaded with swarf. A leak shows low pressure on both sides (see SOP-C-201).

## 3 Immediate action

Reduce coolant demand (lower flow setpoint) and schedule a filter change at the next break.

## 4 Repair

Isolate filter housing. Replace the cartridge. Flush the line. Confirm differential pressure below 0.45 bar and flow back above 38 L/min.
