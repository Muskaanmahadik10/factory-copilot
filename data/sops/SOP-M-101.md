# SOP-M-101: Spindle bearing wear: diagnosis and replacement

## 1 Symptoms

Spindle vibration rising above 3.5 mm/s RMS and trending upward over days; spindle housing temperature 5 C or more above baseline; motor current slightly elevated at constant load; audible noise at high rpm.

## 2 Diagnosis

Confirm with vibration spectrum: bearing defect frequencies present. Check spindle temperature trend against load; wear shows temperature rising without a load increase. Rule out coolant issues (coolant pressure and flow normal).

## 3 Immediate action

Reduce spindle speed to 60 percent. Do not run above 4.5 mm/s vibration. Schedule bearing replacement within 48 hours.

## 4 Replacement procedure

Lock out machine. Remove spindle cartridge. Replace bearing set with matched preload pair. Re-grease to specification. Run in at 30 percent speed for 20 minutes and verify vibration below 2.5 mm/s.
