# SOP-G-001: General alarm response and escalation

## 1 Purpose

Defines who responds to machine alarms and when to escalate.

## 2 Levels

Level 1: operator checks and resets. Level 2: shift lead investigates with the copilot report. Level 3: maintenance engineer required. Level 4: stop production and notify production manager.

## 3 Rule

Any action that stops a line or opens a work order requires approval by the shift lead or above. Automated systems may recommend but not execute such actions.
