# SOP-G-003: Sensor baseline values and alarm limits

## 1 Baselines

Spindle vibration 2.0 mm/s; spindle temperature 48 C; motor current 18 A; coolant temperature 24 C; coolant flow 40 L/min; coolant pressure 3.0 bar; inlet pressure 5.0 bar; outlet pressure 4.6 bar; filter differential pressure 0.4 bar; position error 3 um; rpm 3000.

## 2 Alarm limits

Vibration 3.5 mm/s; spindle temperature 55 C; coolant pressure below 2.6 bar; coolant flow below 36 L/min; filter dP above 0.7 bar; position error above 8 um.
