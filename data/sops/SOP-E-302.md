# SOP-E-302: Axis calibration drift

## 1 Symptoms

Position error increasing slowly and steadily over many cycles, often after a temperature change; parts drifting toward tolerance limits; no spikes.

## 2 Diagnosis

A smooth drift with no spikes and no vibration change is thermal or mechanical drift of the axis, not a sensor fault (see SOP-E-301).

## 3 Immediate action

Continue production if position error is below 8 um; otherwise hold.

## 4 Repair

Run the axis calibration cycle and backlash compensation. Verify with the test artefact; position error must be below 4 um.
