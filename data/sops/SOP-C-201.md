# SOP-C-201: Coolant leak: detection and repair

## 1 Symptoms

Coolant pressure falling below 2.6 bar and coolant flow below 36 L/min together; coolant temperature rising because less fluid is circulating; visible fluid under the machine or at hose fittings.

## 2 Diagnosis

Low pressure and low flow at the same time indicate loss of fluid, not a blockage. A blockage shows high filter differential pressure instead (see SOP-C-202). Inspect hoses, quick-connect fittings and the pump seal.

## 3 Immediate action

Stop the machine. Isolate the coolant pump. Inspect the coolant circuit for leaks and contain spilled fluid.

## 4 Repair

Replace damaged hose or seal. Top up tank to level mark. Restart pump and verify pressure 2.9 to 3.1 bar and flow 38 to 42 L/min.
